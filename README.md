# CIRCASYNC releases

Download the latest CIRCASYNC Android app from the [Releases page](https://github.com/Rohan-Reyes/circasync-releases/releases/latest).

CIRCASYNC is a companion app for a circadian lamp, with a sleep log and to-do list. Once installed, it reminds you when a new version is available.

To install, download `CIRCASYNC.apk` on your Android phone and open it. Your phone may ask you to allow installs from your browser.
